    5 · Investigation missions

    Mission A — Cancel the displacement

    Use Make B = −A. Explain why the resultant has zero magnitude and no defined direction.

    Mission B — Addition order

    Choose any two vectors, note the resultant, then click Swap A and B. What changes in the drawing? What stays the same?

    Mission C — Maximum and minimum

    Keep |A| and |B| fixed. Rotate B until the resultant is maximum, then minimum. What relative angles produce those results?

    Mission D — Two methods, one answer

    Draw an angled A and B, then switch between all three construction methods. Confirm that R stays unchanged.
