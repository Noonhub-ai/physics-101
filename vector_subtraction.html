3 · Step-by-step solution

Vector subtraction means adding the opposite of the second vector. The components of −B have opposite signs to those of B.

    Key geometric insight: When A and B share a tail, A − B is the arrow running from the tip of B to the tip of A. The same resultant can be constructed by placing −B at the tip of A.
