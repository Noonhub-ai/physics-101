The Atwood Machine

Discover why two masses connected by an ideal rope move with equal acceleration magnitudes and share the same tension. Change either mass, release the system, and connect the motion to Newton's Second Law.

01 · Explore

The masses start from rest. Rope is massless and inextensible. Pulley is frictionless with negligible rotational inertia. Motion stops at an idealized travel limit of 1.35 m.
