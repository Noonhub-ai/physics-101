Static & Kinetic Friction

Increase the push on a crate. Watch static friction resist the applied force until sliding begins; then calculate kinetic friction, acceleration, and displacement. All examples use g = 10 m/s².

03 · Friction versus applied force

Purple: static friction magnitude before motion · Brown: kinetic friction after sliding begins · Green dot: your current settings. The abrupt drop is an idealized model.

04 · Calculate

Key rule: Before sliding, static friction is whatever is needed to prevent motion, up to its maximum. After sliding begins, kinetic friction is approximately μₖN.
