Chapter 4 · Laboratory 09

Equilibrium & Tension

A traffic light hangs from two angled supporting cables. Adjust the angles, explore force components, and discover why the object remains at rest even though several forces act on it.

01 · Set up equilibrium

Angles are measured from the horizontal. The lamp is supported by a short vertical cable attached to the central knot.

Explore: Set α = β = 30°, then try α = β = 60°. Which arrangement requires greater tension to support the same weight?

02 · Watch the cables change angle

Static equilibrium: the lamp stays at rest because the net force on the knot is zero.

03 · Resolve forces into components

The left cable pulls upward and left; the right cable pulls upward and right. Their horizontal components cancel while their vertical components support the weight.
