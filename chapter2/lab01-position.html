2. Understand the physics

A position is a coordinate measured relative to a chosen reference point. We choose right as the positive direction.

x = xcar, world − xorigin, world
Step 1. Identify the car's fixed road location: +4 m.
Step 2. Identify the chosen origin: 0 m.
Step 3. Subtract: +4 − 0 = +4 m.

The “world” locations are a fixed measuring grid added for this demonstration. Students would normally describe the object's position using their chosen origin.

Prediction challenge 01

Move the origin, not the car

The car stays at world location +4 m. If the origin moves to +7 m, what will the car's new coordinate be?

Enter your answer before revealing the solution.
Car location = +4 m. New origin = +7 m. x = 4 − 7 = −3 m. Negative means the car lies 3 m to the left of the new origin. The car has not moved.
Prediction challenge 02

Zero does not mean nowhere

A car is located at −6 m on the fixed road. Where should the origin be placed to make the car's coordinate exactly zero?

Try it first.
For the car's relative coordinate to equal zero, 0 = (−6) − xorigin. Therefore xorigin = −6 m. Put the origin at the car's location.
Prediction challenge 03

Can coordinates change without motion?

The car stays in the same physical place, but its coordinate changes from +5 m to −2 m. Did the car necessarily move?

Choose an answer.
No. If the origin shifts 7 m to the right while the car remains still, its coordinate changes by −7 m: +5 − 7 = −2 m. Coordinate changes caused by changing the reference frame are not physical car motion.

3. Takeaway

Position tells us where an object is relative to an origin, not how fast it moves or which way it travels. A negative coordinate means “left of the origin” when right is defined as positive. In the next laboratory, we will distinguish displacement from distance traveled.
