1 · Predict before you explore

Look at the graph in Section 2. The object moves from 3 s to 4 s while its position stays at 5 m. What is its velocity during that interval?

Reveal worked explanation

Velocity is the slope of a position–time graph: v = Δx/Δt = (5 − 5)/(4 − 3) = 0 m/s. A horizontal graph indicates the object is at rest during that interval, although its position is not zero.

3 · Detective questions

Use the lecture graph to investigate each question, then check the final numerical answer.

Question A · Average velocity from 3 s to 4 s
Worked solution

x(3) = 5 m, x(4) = 5 m. So Δx = 5 − 5 = 0 m, Δt = 4 − 3 = 1 s, and v̄ = 0/1 = 0 m/s.

Question B · Instantaneous velocity at 2.5 s
Worked solution

At 2.5 s the car is on the straight segment from (2 s, 10 m) to (3 s, 5 m). Thus v = (5 − 10)/(3 − 2) = −5 m/s. Negative velocity means motion in the −x direction.

Question C · Instantaneous velocity at 1.5 s
Worked solution

From (0 s, 0 m) to (2 s, 10 m), the slope is v = (10 − 0)/(2 − 0) = +5 m/s.

Question D · Distance traveled from 0 s to 8 s
Worked solution

Add the absolute position changes: |10 − 0| + |5 − 10| + |5 − 5| + |0 − 5| + |−5 − 0| + |0 − (−5)| = 10 + 5 + 0 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 30 m. Net displacement is 0 m, which is different from distance.

Bonus: Why is instantaneous velocity not defined at t = 2 s? Inspect the different slopes immediately before and after this corner.

4 · Draw your own motion graph

Your mission: Set the blue point heights for the fixed times 0, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 s to match the target journey below. Drag a point vertically or enter its position using the controls.

Target journey
  1. Start at x = 0 m.
  2. Move to x = 4 m by 2 s.
  3. Remain at x = 4 m until 3 s.
  4. Move to x = −2 m by 4 s.
  5. Stay at x = −2 m until 6 s.
  6. Move to x = 2 m by 7 s.
  7. Return to x = 0 m at 8 s.
Reveal complete solution and segment slopes

The required point heights in order are 0, 4, 4, −2, −2, 2, 0 m.

The six segment velocities are +2, 0, −6, 0, +4, −2 m/s. For example, from t = 3 to 4 s: v = (−2 − 4)/(4 − 3) = −6 m/s.

Total distance = 4 + 0 + 6 + 0 + 4 + 2 = 16 m. Net displacement = 0 − 0 = 0 m.

On a phone, use the Time point and Position controls if dragging the graph is inconvenient. Values snap to 0.5 m increments.

5 · Key concepts to remember

  1. Positive slope: motion in the +x direction.
  2. Negative slope: motion in the −x direction.
  3. Zero slope: object at rest, regardless of where it is.
  4. Steeper slope: greater magnitude of velocity (greater speed).
  5. Sharp corner: idealized abrupt velocity change; instantaneous velocity is undefined there if the slopes differ.
  6. Displacement: final position minus initial position; distance: sum of absolute changes over the full path.
