Challenge A · Stopping time

A car moves right with v₀ = 12 m/s and a = −3 m/s². At what time does it first stop?

Show worked solution

At the turning point, v = 0. Starting with v = v₀ + at:

0 = 12 − 3t → 3t = 12 → t = 4 s.

At that instant the car is momentarily at rest. If acceleration continues, it then moves to the left.

Challenge B · Displacement

A car has v₀ = 5 m/s and a = +2 m/s². How far is its displacement after t = 2.5 s?

Show worked solution

Δx = v₀t + ½at² = 5(2.5) + ½(2)(2.5)² = 12.5 + 6.25.

Δx = 18.75 m.

Challenge C · Speed after five seconds

A car starts from rest and reaches 5 m/s in 3 s, with constant acceleration. What speed will it have after 5 s?

Show worked solution

First find a: 5 = 0 + a(3), so a = 5/3 m/s². Then v = 0 + (5/3)(5).

Speed = 25/3 ≈ 8.33 m/s.

Challenge D · Distance versus displacement after reversing

A car starts at x₀ = 0 with v₀ = +4 m/s and a = −2 m/s². After 5 s, find both displacement and total distance traveled.

Show worked solution

Turning time t* = −v₀/a = 2 s. At t* the car reaches x = 4(2) − (2)² = +4 m. At 5 s: x = 4(5) − (5)² = −5 m.

Displacement = −5 − 0 = −5 m. Distance = |4 − 0| + |−5 − 4| = 4 + 9 = 13 m.
