5 · Predict before revealing

Challenge A — Which way is it moving?

For x(t) = 2 + 3t − t², what is the instantaneous velocity at t = 1 s?

Show worked solution

Differentiate position: v(t) = dx/dt = 3 − 2t.

Substitute t = 1 s: v(1) = 3 − 2(1) = +1 m/s. Positive means motion toward the right (+x).

Challenge B — Average velocity

For x(t) = 3t − 4t² + 2, find average velocity between t = 1 s and t = 2 s.

Show worked solution

x(1) = 3 − 4 + 2 = 1 m; x(2) = 6 − 16 + 2 = −8 m.

v̄ = [−8 − 1]/[2 − 1] = −9 m/s.

The negative sign indicates net motion toward −x over this interval.

Challenge C — Find the turning point

For x(t) = 2 + 3t − t², at what time does the particle reverse direction?

Show worked solution

At the turning point, v = 0. Since v(t) = 3 − 2t, solve 0 = 3 − 2t.

t = 1.5 s. Position at that instant: x(1.5) = 2 + 4.5 − 2.25 = 4.25 m.

6 · Understand the slope

  1. The slope of a secant through two points on the position–time graph is average velocity: v̄ = [x(t₂) − x(t₁)]/(t₂ − t₁).
  2. As B approaches A, the average velocity approaches the instantaneous velocity at A: v = dx/dt.
  3. A horizontal tangent means v = 0 at that instant. It does not necessarily mean the particle remains at rest.
  4. The area under an x–t graph is not displacement. Displacement is the change in position; we will explore area under v–t graphs in Lab 06.

At exactly t₁ = t₂, the secant formula gives 0/0 and is undefined. We therefore show “undefined” for average velocity there; the tangent slope still exists and is obtained using the derivative.
