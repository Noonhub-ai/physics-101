2 · Predict before you move

Scenario: Start at x = 0 m. Move to x = +20 m, then back to x = 0 m. Each trip takes 2 s.

Reveal step-by-step explanation
Step 1: Outbound distance = |20 − 0| = 20 m.
Step 2: Return distance = |0 − 20| = 20 m.
Step 3: Total distance = 20 + 20 = 40 m.
Step 4: Displacement = xf − xi = 0 − 0 = 0 m.
Step 5: Total time = 4 s; average speed = 40/4 = 10 m/s; average velocity = 0/4 = 0 m/s.

What do the numbers mean?

Distance (scalar)

Add the magnitudes of all changes in position:

D = Σ |xnext − xprevious|

Never negative. It measures the total path length.

Displacement (signed)

Compare only where you started and ended:

Δx = xf − xi

Positive, negative, or zero.

Average quantities

Average speed = total distance / total elapsed time.

Average velocity = displacement / total elapsed time.

3 · Your turn: self-check

A particle moves from x = −10 m to x = +15 m, then returns to x = +5 m. What are its total distance and net displacement?

Detailed solution
First leg: |15 − (−10)| = 25 m.
Second leg: |5 − 15| = 10 m.
Distance = 25 + 10 = 35 m.
Displacement = 5 − (−10) = +15 m.
